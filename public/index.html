WHAT IS
WODAROO?

Wodaroo is CrossFit Trivium's premier annual fitness competition, built to deliver a high-energy, professionally run experience for athletes, judges, spectators, and partners.

It's a full-day event where performance, community, and brand presence come together.

Hosted in the heart of the Nashville/Brentwood fitness community, Wodaroo brings together top local athletes, hundreds of spectators, and a culture that values hard work, connection, and showing up.
